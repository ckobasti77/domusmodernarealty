# 04: Content, voice, glossary, compliance

## Brand voice
Calm, precise, confident, warm. We speak like a trusted advisor, not a salesman. Short sentences, concrete facts (m², floor, heating, registration), no hype words ("najbolji", "neverovatno", "jedinstveno" only if true), no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- **Serbian:** Latin script, formal "Vi" capitalized in direct address on forms ("Vaša poruka"), natural Belgrade usage ("dvosoban", "uknjižen", "CG").
- **English:** British-leaning, understated. **Russian:** formal "Вы", standard terms (see glossary). **German:** "Sie", precise.
- Translations must read as native, not literal. Keep brand and neighborhood names untranslated. In ru, transliterate neighborhood names in Cyrillic (Врачар, Дорчол, Сеньяк, Дедине, Нови-Белград, Белград на воде, Земун).

## Compliance (Serbia)
- **Brokerage law (Zakon o posredovanju u prometu i zakupu nepokretnosti):**
  - **Art. 18:** every ad shows the agency's business name + registry number (Registar posrednika), price, location, area and room structure. No advertising without a signed brokerage agreement.
  - **Art. 16:** the agent checks the ownership documents. Hence the "Verifikovana dokumentacija" badge only when `docsVerifiedAt` is set.
  - General terms incl. commission must be available, as a page plus a PDF from settings.
- **ZZPL (GDPR-aligned):**
  - Every form states its purpose + legal basis and links to the privacy policy.
  - Marketing needs a separate unticked opt-in.
  - Store the consent record (text version, timestamp, channel).
  - Support access/erasure on request (admin "anonymize contact").
- **Cookies:** opt-in banner for non-essential cookies; analytics loads only after consent.
- **E-commerce law, Art. 6:** company data in the footer (name, address, PIB, MB, contact).
- **Legal pages:** seed as clearly marked drafts ("NACRT — proveriti sa pravnikom pre objave").

## Demo content rules
- Demo listings/agents/posts/stats/testimonials all have `isDemo: true`.
- **Demo agents:**
  - Plausible Serbian names with monogram avatars (no photos of real people).
  - Mark them in admin as "Demo".
  - Use obviously fictional contact details: phones `+381 11 000 00xx`, emails `@mihajlo-estate.local`.
- Demo testimonials are `verified: false`, so they never render publicly.
- Listing texts must be specific and realistic: street character, light, orientation, transport, schools, building state, what is included. Vary structure and length. No lorem ipsum anywhere.
