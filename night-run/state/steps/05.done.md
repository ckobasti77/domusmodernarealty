# Step 05 done — Animated landing page

## Summary

`/[locale]` is now the full landing from DESIGN §6: preloader, hero, intro
statement, featured listings, neighborhoods, services, 3D-tours teaser,
process, numbers, valuation CTA, journal, testimonials (verified-only, so absent
on the demo data) and the final CTA flowing into the footer. Every figure,
listing, place, post and contact channel is read from Convex on the server in
one parallel round (`components/site/home/data.ts::loadHomeData`); the client
islands only animate what the server already rendered. All copy is localized in
sr-Latn / en / ru / de (the Russian and English home copy was re-translated
against the current Serbian source by the i18n-translator agent).

Choreography follows §6 with three branches everywhere (`gsap.matchMedia`):
desktop (≥1024, motion allowed) pins the featured rail and the 3D-tours zoom and
scrubs the intro words and the process rail; phones get their own lighter
entrances, a snap carousel and image cards; reduced motion paints final states
with no pin and no Lenis (and gets explicit step buttons for the rail).

Owner media were already optimized (`public/media/**`); the mapping was
confirmed by viewing every render and is logged in DECISIONS.md. The hero uses
the owner's blue-hour render (desktop) / 9:16 render (portrait) under a slow
Ken Burns move. The owner's hero film was withdrawn after critique pass 4
because it shows an invented tower under the "Beograd" eyebrow (see Decisions).

The `design-critic` agent ran four passes:
- pass 1: 4 High, 10 Medium, 6 Low (score 6/10);
- pass 2: 2 High, 7 Medium — a first-visit banner collision introduced by the
  CLS fix, and upscaled card photos;
- pass 3: 1 High (the owner's aerial film shows Budapest), 3 Medium;
- pass 4: see "Critique" below.
Every High and Medium from passes 1–3 was fixed and re-verified in the pixels.

## Decisions

All in `docs/DECISIONS.md` (entries tagged `[05]`). The ones later steps must know:

1. **Owner media mapping** and why phones never play the hero film or any
   ambient video.
2. **The hero media layer is clipped 1px short of the viewport and capped at
   `100svh - 1px`.** Chrome drops a full-viewport image as an LCP candidate;
   without this the LCP was the animated headline at ~2 s.
3. **The cookie banner's height is reserved in the hero from the first paint**
   (`html[data-consent="pending"]` → `--consent-reserve`), the banner is compact
   and waits for the intro. Padding by the banner's measured height (step 04's
   approach) caused CLS 0.15–0.35.
4. **Demo photos are fetched pre-cropped to their frame** (imgix loader) and
   editorial photos get `photo-grade`; listing photos are never graded.
5. **The neighborhoods band is a verified Belgrade photograph**; the owner's
   `city.mp4` renders Budapest and is unused.
6. Services rows reveal on hover without moving anything (CLS), text always
   shown on touch.
7. `listings.featured` dedupes covers; the index shows DESIGN §6.5's places and
   keeps every listing count.
8. The listing page's `PriceBlock` gets `tone="light"` (a pre-run baseline
   change had made the dark tone the default on an ivory header — axe failed
   every listing).
9. **Both owner films are withdrawn by the SHA-256 of their source file**
   (`WITHDRAWN` in `scripts/optimize-media.mjs`). `hero.mp4` ends on an
   invented Moscow-style tower and hard-cuts its loop; `city.mp4` shows
   Budapest. With no film in the manifest, `HeroStage` runs the still with Ken
   Burns at every width. A corrected re-render saved under the same name in
   `media-src/` has a new hash and is picked up by `npm run media:optimize`
   with no code change.
10. On a first visit the 3D-tours stage is shortened by the banner reservation,
    so its frame and CTA stay above the banner. The featured rail is not (see
    Known issues).

## Known issues

- **Both owner films are withdrawn and need a re-render.** `hero.mp4` pans onto
  an invented tiered tower (and hard-cuts its loop); `city.mp4` shows Budapest
  landmarks. Ask the owner for Belgrade-referenced re-renders with a seamless
  loop. Dropped into `media-src/` under the same names, they are used again
  after `npm run media:optimize` (the hero film plays on landscape screens
  after `load`; the poster stays the LCP).
- **On a first visit the cookie banner overlays the bottom strip of the pinned
  featured rail** (meta row, listing code, "01 / 06" counter) until the visitor
  answers the banner. Shortening the pin made the counter collide with the
  cards, and at laptop heights (650–790 px) the 4:5 cards would have had to
  shrink to ~180 px. If this matters, a slimmer "scrolling" state of the banner
  (one line + two buttons) is the proper fix.
- German copy can still end a line on the article "das" (pass-4 L4):
  `fixTypography` has no German article rule yet.
- A stale walkthrough poster can survive in `.next/cache/images` after
  `media:optimize` re-renders it under the same name. Clear that folder (done
  in this step) or version the file name when a poster changes.
- **The dev deployment was force re-seeded in this step** (`seed:all
  {"force":true}`) to apply the new featured places, covers and untranslated
  "Novi Beograd". Listing ids changed; slugs did not.
- **Convex-storage uploads in 4:5 landing cards** are still requested at the
  box width (only Unsplash photos get the crop loader); step 08 should store a
  4:5 rendition of the cover on upload.
- **Card titles longer than ~52 characters are clipped** at a word boundary
  with "…" (full title kept for screen readers). Content-side short titles are
  the better fix; the admin (step 08) should show a character counter.
- **Every demo card carries "3D TURA"**; one badge per card on the landing is a
  search/card decision for step 06.
- **Novi Sad has no context line** in the index (it is a city without a parent
  region in the data).
- **Links from the landing to routes later steps build** (`/nekretnine`,
  `/procena`, `/prodajte`, `/kontakt`, `/lokacije/*`, `/magazin/*`) 404 until
  then; they carry `prefetch={false}` per the step-04 decision. The search
  (`transaction`, `location`, `priceMax`, `hasTour`) and valuation (`location`,
  `area`) query contracts are in `lib/search-params.ts`.
- The `review-animations` and `high-end-visual-design` skills are
  user-invocable only; `motion-design`, the `gsap-*` set, `frontend-design` and
  `core-web-vitals` were used instead.
- No TODO comments were left in the codebase.
