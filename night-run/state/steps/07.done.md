## Decisions

All are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` as `[07]` entries dated 2026-09-30:

- server-side Belgrade time, with no client offset;
- a web viewing is one step of the hourly grid, at least 2 h ahead and at most 14 days out, with one open request per address and listing;
- per-address rate limits are the real control, and the global windows are flood breakers;
- off-market listings take no leads;
- an agency contact card replaces a hidden agent;
- POI rounding, a published-only price history, and no `views` in the public DTO;
- `hasTour` means a tour the page can open;
- a 12 s map load deadline;
- never put two `text-*` utilities on one element.
