## Decisions

All are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` as `[09]` entries dated 2026-10-01. In brief:

- **Shared admin patterns:** the open record lives in the URL (`?id=`).
- **Uploads:** one staff upload URL, with server validation where the blob is linked. Blobs must be at most 6 h old when linked. Deletes are safe for missing blobs.
- **Activity diffs** decide "changed" on full values.
- **Lead pipeline:** index-driven filters, keyboard move menu, mandatory loss reason. Logs carry no free text.
- **Public forms never edit an existing contact.** The submitted phone and consent live on the lead; owner contacts are excluded from matching.
- **Anonymization** is complete, batched and self-rescheduling. Rate-limit keys are hashed.
- **CSV:** `;` separator + BOM, 2,000 rows max, formula-safe, logged in the same transaction.
- **Viewings:** transitions enforced on the server. Reassigning a lead moves its open viewings. Contact data only for the lead's owner.
- **Locations:** slug changes are warned about, not redirected. Delete and move are refused while the location is in use.
- **Invites without e-mail:** a copyable one-time link plus renew.
- **Demo profiles:** an adopted profile stops being demo, and the purge skips profiles in use. A hidden agent gets the slug `agencija` publicly.
- **Journal scheduling** uses the Convex scheduler (a draft with `scheduledAt`).
- **Landing copy:** texts come from `pages.home`; eyebrows, alts and buttons stay in `messages`. The seed was regenerated from `messages`.
- **Templates:** legal pages are free-form; other pages are fixed templates.
- **Demo figures and testimonials:** only admin/manager may mark figures real or verify a testimonial, and verifying is tied to the reviewed text.
- **Settings:** section-wise saves. The legal save ends demo status.
- **Activity log:** one vocabulary, shared with the dashboard. Managers don't see user/invite rows or staff e-mails.
- **Status counts:** exact listing counters replace the capped scans. This fixes step 08 known issue M5.

## Known issues

- **OpenFreeMap** was still not reached from this machine. The location centroid picker falls back to its "unavailable" notice after 12 s; the lat/lng inputs always work. The pin drag was not exercised in a browser.
- **Locale switchers:** each localized field has its own SR/EN/RU/DE switcher. Translating a long page means many clicks (design critique M5). A page-level switcher would be better.
- **Long Sadržaj page:** the home page editor is long (about 9,000 px at 1440); blocks are not collapsible (critique M6).
- **Two toolbar patterns:** filters above the card (step 09) and inside the card (step 08 Nekretnine). They are not unified, and phone filters take vertical space (critique M7/M8).
- **Upiti has no text search and no "Novi upit"** (manual phone/walk-in lead). PRD B4 doesn't require them; the subtitle no longer promises them.
- **Viewings sheet has no "Potvrdi termin" button;** status changes go through the select (critique M12).
- **Location slugs:** changing a slug breaks old `/lokacije/<slug>` links. There is no redirect table for locations.
- **Tour assets:** `toursAdmin` re-validates panoramas and splats on every save, so they are not under the 6 h link window. A colleague's old blob id could still be attached as a tour asset. Step 11 should apply the "only new/changed" rule there.
- **Blob ownership:** this is still enforced only by the 6 h window plus not exposing storage ids, not by an ownership ledger. Orphaned uploads (abandoned forms) are not garbage-collected.
- **Invites:** re-inviting with "create profile" can leave a superseded hidden profile behind (auditor L6). `qa:purgeE2EUser` now removes e2e ones.
- **Analytics:** `leadsOverTime` and `responseTimes` read bounded index ranges and flag `capped` in the UI when a cap is hit.
- **Viewing caps:** the agency-wide viewings calendar reads at most 500 rows per range, and the locations tree reads at most 500 per kind.
- **E2E leftovers:** the suite leaves `activity` rows (append-only by design). Pages are restored exactly from a snapshot. The e2e lead, post, user and invite are purged.
- **Settings:** the dev deployment's settings are still demo (PIB/MB/registry "0…"). The seeded insurance placeholder text was removed, from the seed and from the dev settings.
- **Public settings not yet used:** office hours, terms PDF, office coordinates, default SEO/OG and analytics IDs are editable but not read by public pages yet. That is step 10/12 work.
- **shadcn registry:** unreachable (`ui.shadcn.com` timeout), so no new shadcn components were added.
- No TODO comments were left in the code.
