## Decisions

All are in DECISIONS.md under `[15]`. The ones later work should know:

- Staff change their own password through `users.changeOwnPassword`. It uses the library's `retrieveAccount`, so it shares the sign-in lockout, and it ends the account's other sessions.
- The demo purge keeps the privacy, terms and cookie pages and does not count them.
- The first-visit consent decision keeps the hero's reservation for that page view. The banner slides out in 200 ms.
- The SplitText line-mask geometry is identical split and unsplit. Never stack negative block margins on the masks again.
- Curtain timing: the lift is 1 s on `cubic-bezier(.76,0,.24,1)` from 0.8 s, done by 1.8 s. This is deliberately not the spec's 1.1 s `expo.inOut`, which whipped.
- Card badges and the scrim sit at z-12, above the touch photo strip. 3:2 titles clamp at 3 lines, 4:5 at 2. Map clusters use a 60 px radius on the pin centre line, and framing clears the chip stack.
- `coverPhoto` in a seed spec pins one of the listing's own planned photos. The displaced cover is left out, and no other listing's photos change.
- The Art. 18 line uses the registered business name ("Poslovno ime"), with the brand as a fallback.
- The detail gallery opens on the cover staff chose.
